# Contribution Statement Examples

Use these patterns to turn generic contribution claims into theory-facing claims.

## Generic

This study enriches the literature on digital transformation and firm performance.

## Mechanism

This study contributes to digital transformation research by specifying the attentional mechanism through which [DIGITAL PHENOMENON] shapes [ORGANIZATIONAL OUTCOME].

## Boundary Condition

This study extends [THEORY] by showing that its core prediction depends on [BOUNDARY CONDITION], which changes the extent to which [MECHANISM] translates into [OUTCOME].

## Integration

By integrating [THEORY A] and [THEORY B], this study explains why [PHENOMENON] produces both [PATH 1] and [PATH 2], thereby reconciling mixed evidence on [RELATIONSHIP].

## Contextualization

This study contextualizes [EMERGING BUSINESS PHENOMENON] within [THEORY] by showing how [CONTEXTUAL FEATURE] alters the assumptions under which prior explanations operate.
