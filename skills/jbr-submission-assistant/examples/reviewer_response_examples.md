# Reviewer Response Examples

Use these examples for tone and structure.

## Clarifying Theory

Comment: The theoretical contribution is unclear.

Response: Thank you for identifying this issue. We revised the introduction and discussion to state the contribution more directly. The manuscript now explains that the study contributes to [LITERATURE] by [SPECIFIC CONTRIBUTION], rather than presenting the findings as a general extension of prior work. We also removed broader claims that were not supported by the empirical design.

## Softening Causal Language

Comment: The manuscript overstates causality.

Response: We agree and have revised the manuscript to calibrate the claim strength to the research design. We replaced causal wording with association-based language throughout the abstract, results, and discussion, and we added a limitation noting that the design cannot fully rule out [ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATION].

## Partial Adoption

Comment: Add a new analysis to address endogeneity.

Response: Thank you for this constructive suggestion. We added [ANALYSIS] to address [THREAT]. Because [DATA LIMITATION] prevents us from implementing [REQUESTED METHOD] cleanly, we also revised the limitations section to state this boundary more explicitly and softened the related claims in the discussion.
