# JBR Method Checklists

Use this reference for methods, results, robustness, and claim-evidence alignment.

## Universal Method Fit

Check every empirical manuscript for:

- Construct definitions before measures.
- Construct-to-measure fit.
- Level-of-analysis consistency across theory, variables, and models.
- Sampling frame and sample construction transparency.
- Rationale for empirical setting.
- Missing data, exclusions, and transformations.
- Model choice aligned with research question and data structure.
- Alternative explanations and reviewer concerns.
- Robustness checks targeted to threats rather than added mechanically.
- Claims calibrated to identification strength.

## Quantitative Archival or Survey

Required checks:

- Variables clearly defined as dependent, independent, mediator, moderator, controls, and fixed effects.
- Measure sources, calculation windows, coding rules, and citations are explicit.
- Time ordering supports the argument where possible.
- Endogeneity concerns are named and handled proportionately.
- Common method bias is addressed for survey-heavy designs.
- Reliability, validity, factor structure, and discriminant validity are reported for multi-item constructs.
- Robustness checks test plausible threats: alternative measures, model forms, lag structures, influential observations, omitted variables, selection, reverse causality, and fixed effects.

For archival panel manuscripts, also load `references/methods/archival_panel_checklist.md`. Use that file for sample construction, panel timing, fixed-effects architecture, text/AI-measure validation, endogeneity threats, and robustness sequencing. Do not rely on this generic checklist alone for archival panel designs.

Results narrative:

- Report descriptive statistics and correlations first.
- Present tests in hypothesis order.
- Interpret substantive meaning, not only significance.
- Explain interactions, nonlinear effects, or marginal effects with figures when useful.
- Keep robustness tied to threats.

## Experiments

Required checks:

- Random assignment, manipulation, procedure, sample, and exclusions are transparent.
- Manipulation checks and attention checks are justified.
- Measures match the theoretical constructs.
- Statistical tests correspond to experimental design.
- Mediation claims use appropriate caution if process evidence is indirect.
- External validity and boundary conditions are acknowledged.

## Case or Qualitative Research

Required checks:

- Case selection logic is theoretical, not merely access-based.
- Data sources are triangulated.
- Interview, archive, observation, and coding procedures are transparent.
- Coding moves from first-order evidence to second-order themes to aggregate dimensions.
- Evidence tables support the theoretical claims.
- The manuscript avoids becoming an organizational story without abstraction.
- Boundary conditions are clear.

## Review Articles

Required checks:

- Clear review question.
- Search databases, search strings, inclusion/exclusion criteria, period, and screening process.
- Coding framework and reliability.
- Synthesizing framework that produces insight, not only categorization.
- Future research agenda organized around theoretical gaps, method gaps, context gaps, and boundary conditions.

## Conceptual Papers

Required checks:

- Clear phenomenon or theoretical puzzle.
- Definitions and scope conditions.
- Proposition logic builds progressively.
- The model changes understanding rather than relabeling known relationships.
- Practical implications follow from the conceptual model.

## Mixed Methods

Required checks:

- Each method has a distinct role.
- Integration logic is explicit: exploratory, explanatory, triangulation, or complementarity.
- The sequence and weighting of methods match the claims.
- Qualitative and quantitative evidence answer the same theoretical question.

## Claim-Evidence Calibration

Use these replacements when evidence is weaker than rhetoric:

- Replace "demonstrates" with "suggests" when the design is correlational.
- Replace "causes" with "is associated with" unless identification supports causality.
- Replace "mechanism" with "theorized mechanism" when not directly tested.
- Replace "proves" with "provides evidence consistent with."
- Replace universal claims with context-bound claims when the sample is narrow.
