# UTD24 5-Dimension Rubric — scoring details

This file is the **scoring authority** for IDEA, MANUSCRIPT, and REVIEW modes. Every score must trace back to a sub-criterion here. Do not invent new dimensions or sub-criteria at runtime.

Each dimension has **4 sub-criteria**, each worth 5 points (4 × 5 = 20). Total per dimension: 0-20. Total per manuscript: 0-100.

For each sub-criterion: **5 points** = UTD24 anchor, **3 points** = marginal, **1 point** = below UTD24 floor, **0 points** = absent or fatal.

---

## Dim 1 — Research Question (0-20)

### 1.1 Why-now answer (timeliness / phenomenon-level urgency) — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | RQ ties to a *specific* recent phenomenon, regulatory shift, technology, or market event with quantitative or near-quantitative scope (e.g., "the post-2020 surge in generative-AI deployment in TMTs", "the 2018-2022 wave of SPAC IPOs", "the post-pandemic remote-work natural experiment"). User can name 1-2 concrete external events. |
| 3 | RQ alludes to "growing interest" or "increasing attention" without naming the trigger. |
| 1 | RQ has no timeliness hook — could have been written 15 years ago. |
| 0 | RQ is fashion-chasing (e.g., "X meets AI") without theoretical urgency. |

### 1.2 Why-care answer (theoretical stakes) — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | RQ explicitly states which theoretical conversation it advances and what changes if the answer is X vs Y. The "so what" answer is non-trivial and not "we'd know more about X". |
| 3 | Theoretical stakes mentioned but generic ("contributes to the strategy literature"). |
| 1 | Stakes are policy-level / managerial-level only, with no theoretical change. |
| 0 | No stakes articulated. |

### 1.3 Scope calibration — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Scope is one mechanism × one boundary × one outcome. Not a literature review masquerading as a paper. Not a single-finding replication. |
| 3 | Scope is two mechanisms OR two outcomes OR multi-construct — feasible but ambitious. |
| 1 | Scope is "everything about X" or "one variable's effect on another in our sample" — sprawl or replication. |
| 0 | Scope is so narrow it's a methods paper or so broad it's a textbook chapter. |

### 1.4 Question phrasing (interrogative, not declarative) — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | RQ is a proper question (How / Why / When / Under what conditions). Not "We examine X" or "This paper studies X". |
| 3 | RQ is a stated *aim* not a question — fixable. |
| 1 | RQ is buried in the intro, never explicitly phrased. |
| 0 | No RQ identifiable. |

## Dim 2 — Literature Conversation (0-20)

### 2.1 Parent theory named, not gestured — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Parent theory named by lineage (e.g., "Adner-Kapoor ecosystem framework", "attention-based view", "Carnegie behavioral tradition") with 2-3 specific anchor papers. |
| 3 | Parent theory is a broad school ("dynamic capabilities literature") with anchor papers but no specific position within the lineage stated. |
| 1 | Cites "the strategy literature" or "the innovation literature" as the conversation. |
| 0 | No parent theory identifiable. |

### 2.2 Anchor papers are recent + central — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | 2-3 anchor papers, at least one published in last 5 years in a UTD24 outlet (or AMR / Strategy Science / equivalent). Papers are central to the lineage, not peripheral. |
| 3 | Anchor papers exist but only one is recent OR only one is central. |
| 1 | Anchor papers are 15+ years old OR are not in the named lineage. |
| 0 | No anchor papers cited. |

### 2.3 Contribution framing — 5pts

The contribution must be framed as a *theoretical movement*, not a gap-fill.

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Contribution is one of: EXTENSION (adds a new condition / mediator / moderator to existing theory) / INTEGRATION (joins two previously separate conversations with a defensible bridge) / RECONCILIATION (resolves a documented tension between findings) / BOUNDARY (specifies when theory breaks) / MECHANISM (specifies a previously unspecified causal pathway) / NEW-THEORY (rare; requires AMR-grade exposition). |
| 3 | Framing claims one of the above but the move itself is small ("we add one more moderator"). |
| 1 | Framing is "we fill a gap in the literature" or "no prior study has examined X" without specifying what theoretical change results. |
| 0 | Framing is "we extend the literature" with no specified theoretical change. |

### 2.4 Conversation visibility (Who, exactly, is the paper arguing with?) — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Paper names 2-3 specific recent papers it is in dialogue with — both supporting and challenging. Reader can immediately picture the conversation. |
| 3 | Paper engages 1 specific recent paper; rest is generic. |
| 1 | Paper cites many recent papers as a list without distinguishing supporters vs challengers. |
| 0 | Paper does not engage any specific recent paper. |

## Dim 3 — Theoretical Mechanism (0-20)

### 3.1 Causal pathway named and directional — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Mechanism is a *named* causal pathway with a clear direction (X → M → Y where M is specified). Reader can articulate the pathway after one read. |
| 3 | Mechanism is gestured ("we argue X affects Y through M") but M is generic ("legitimacy", "cognition", "learning") without specifying which subdimension. |
| 1 | Mechanism is "X and Y are related because [theory] says so". |
| 0 | No mechanism — just correlational expectation. |

### 3.2 Falsifiability — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | At least one prediction is stated such that observing the opposite would force theory revision. The mechanism rules out a specific alternative. |
| 3 | Predictions are directional but symmetric (the theory could fit either result). |
| 1 | Predictions are "X is associated with Y" with no specified shape or boundary. |
| 0 | Mechanism is consistent with any plausible outcome — unfalsifiable. |

### 3.3 Alternative explanations addressed — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | At least 2 alternative explanations explicitly named and ruled out theoretically (or method addresses them). E.g., "this is not simply selection because...", "this is not legitimacy-seeking because...". |
| 3 | One alternative explanation is named. |
| 1 | "Alternative explanations" mentioned generically, none named. |
| 0 | No engagement with alternatives. |

### 3.4 Analogy is not substituting for mechanism — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Analogies (if used) serve as illustration of a fully-specified mechanism, not as substitute for one. The mechanism stands without the analogy. |
| 3 | Analogy is load-bearing but the named pathway exists, just under-specified. |
| 1 | Mechanism is "similar to X in setting Y" — analogy is doing the work. |
| 0 | Mechanism is entirely "akin to" / "parallels" / "mirrors" — analogy-as-mechanism. Hard Rule 4 triggers DESK-REJECT-LEVEL flag. |

## Dim 4 — Hypothesis Architecture (0-20)

### 4.1 Per-hypothesis 3-layer chain — 5pts

Each hypothesis must contain: (a) mechanism layer (why this causal direction), (b) boundary layer (under what conditions), (c) counter-argument layer (why the opposite might be expected, and why we predict the stated direction).

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Every hypothesis has all three layers. |
| 3 | Most hypotheses have mechanism + boundary; counter-argument is missing or perfunctory. |
| 1 | Hypotheses have mechanism only — no boundaries, no counter-arguments. |
| 0 | Hypotheses are stated as predictions without theoretical scaffolding. |

### 4.2 Hypothesis count — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | 3-5 hypotheses. Each does theoretical work, none is throwaway. |
| 3 | 6-7 hypotheses, all defensible but scope creep is visible. |
| 1 | 8+ hypotheses, or 1-2 hypotheses (under-developed). |
| 0 | Hypotheses inflated to perform "comprehensiveness" — multiple redundant variants of the same prediction. |

### 4.3 Mechanism-outcome alignment — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | The construct being measured for the outcome is the *exact* construct the mechanism predicts. No "we argue about X, we measure Y as a proxy" without explicit defense. |
| 3 | Outcome is a defensible proxy with stated assumptions. |
| 1 | Outcome is a loose proxy with no defense. |
| 0 | Outcome is a different construct from what the mechanism predicts. |

### 4.4 HARKing-risk indicators — 5pts (binary-ish: 5 or 0, with intermediate for soft flags)

Indicators of Hypothesizing After Results are Known:

- Hypotheses suspiciously match unusual mediator / moderator findings without prior theoretical reason
- Theory section written in past tense as if reporting findings
- Predicted direction was not stated before the result was observed (no pre-registration if experiment)
- Hypotheses include odd interaction terms with no a priori justification

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | No HARKing indicators. Pre-registration filed if experiment. |
| 3 | One soft indicator. |
| 1 | Multiple soft indicators OR one hard indicator. |
| 0 | Hard HARKing pattern. DESK-REJECT-LEVEL flag per Hard Rule 12. |

## Dim 5 — Methods & Identification (0-20)

### 5.1 Identification strategy supports claim verb — 5pts

Match between method tier and causal language:

| Method tier | Allowed verbs |
|---|---|
| Cross-sectional survey | associated with, correlated with, predicts |
| Panel (no exogenous shock) | associated with, predicts, persistently associated with |
| Panel + DiD with parallel-trends evidence | causes (in the DiD sample), drives |
| RCT / lab experiment | causes (within ecological-validity caveats) |
| IV with credible exclusion | causes (subject to LATE interpretation) |
| Regression discontinuity | causes (at the cutoff) |
| Formal theory | proves (within model assumptions), proposes |
| Qual case | suggests, illuminates, develops theory about |

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Claim verbs match the identification table everywhere. |
| 3 | Most claims match; abstract/intro overshoots methods. |
| 1 | Claim verbs systematically overshoot identification. |
| 0 | Cross-sectional design making "causes" claims. Hard Rule 3 triggers. |

### 5.2 Construct validity — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | All key constructs use measures validated in prior published work (with citation), OR new measures include convergent + discriminant validity evidence in the manuscript. |
| 3 | Most constructs validated; one new construct introduced with face-validity argument only. |
| 1 | Multiple constructs introduced without validation. |
| 0 | Critical construct (DV or key IV) is freshly invented with no validation. UTD24 reviewers will reject. |

### 5.3 Robustness coverage — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | At least 3 robustness checks targeting different threats (alternative DV, alternative IV, subsample, alternative model, alternative identification). Reported in main text or appendix. |
| 3 | 1-2 robustness checks present. |
| 1 | Robustness mentioned but only "results are similar" without specifics. |
| 0 | No robustness section. |

### 5.4 Alternative-explanation rule-outs (empirical) — 5pts

| Score | Anchor |
|---:|---|
| 5 | At least 2 alternative explanations from Dim 3.3 are empirically tested (not just theoretically dismissed). E.g., test for selection, test for reverse causality, test for confounding mechanism. |
| 3 | One alternative explanation empirically tested. |
| 1 | Alternatives mentioned in discussion, not tested. |
| 0 | No engagement with alternative explanations in methods/results. |

## Outlet-conditioned floor (apply on top of the 5-dim total)

UTD24-strict outlets do **not** share a uniform bar. A 80/100 total can be "send out" at one outlet and "desk reject" at another, because each outlet weights different dimensions and has different decisive failure modes. After computing the 5-dim total, apply the **per-dim floor** for the user's target outlet. If any dim falls below the outlet's per-dim floor, the verdict downgrades regardless of total.

| Outlet | Dim 1 floor | Dim 2 floor | Dim 3 floor | Dim 4 floor | Dim 5 floor | Decisive failure mode | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| **SMJ** | 15 | 16 | 16 | 16 | **18** | Weak identification — Dim 5 carries the most weight; clean DiD / IV / RDD is near-required for causal claims | Most identification-strict UTD24 outlet for archival work |
| **AMJ** | 15 | 16 | **17** | **17** | 15 | Weak mechanism or thin hypothesis architecture — R1 will demand 3-layer chain everywhere | Mid-balanced; mechanism + hypothesis rigor critical |
| **ASQ** | 16 | 17 | **18** | 15 | 14 | Weak theoretical depth or thin mechanism — "richness" trumps clean identification | Most demanding on Dim 3 depth; qual papers calibrated to richness, not identification |
| **OS** | **17** | 17 | **18** | 15 | 13 | Lack of originality or derivative theoretical move — Dim 1 + Dim 3 carry weight; "interesting" matters more than "clean" | Originality-weighted; can accept weaker Dim 5 if theory is genuinely new |
| **MS (Strategy/Innovation/Entrepreneurship section)** | 15 | 15 | 16 | 16 | **18** | Weak identification OR formal model rigor — quantitatively rigorous outlet | Identification or formal rigor decisive; computational simulation favored |
| **AMR** | 16 | **18** | **18** | 14† | N/A‡ | Insufficient theoretical contribution or weak engagement with parent theory — only pure-theory papers, no empirics | †Dim 4 reframed as "proposition architecture" (not hypotheses) ‡Dim 5 not assessed; substitute "logical consistency of formal/conceptual argument" scored as 0-20 |
| **Strategy Science** | 16 | 17 | **18** | 15 | 16 | Lack of mechanism originality OR weak experimental/formal rigor — UTD24-adjacent, breakthrough-skewed | Breakthrough originality + clean evidence; favors lab experiments with executives, formal/computational models |

### How to apply the outlet floor

1. Compute the 5-dim score normally.
2. Identify the user's target outlet (from Intake Gate field "Target outlet preference").
3. For each dim, check the manuscript's score against the outlet's per-dim floor in the table above.
4. **If any dim falls below the outlet floor**, downgrade the verdict regardless of total:
   - If 1 dim below floor → `MARGINAL — outlet-specific gap on Dim X`
   - If 2+ dims below floor → `NEEDS-MAJOR — outlet mismatch`
   - If decisive failure mode triggered (e.g., Dim 5 < 12 at SMJ; Dim 3 < 12 at ASQ) → `DESK-REJECT RISK HIGH at target outlet`
5. Report the outlet floor analysis explicitly: "Total 82/100 ostensibly send-out, but Dim 5 = 14 < SMJ floor of 18 → MARGINAL at SMJ, would be STRONG at AMJ (Dim 5 floor 15)."

### When the user has not picked an outlet

If the user said "undecided" in Intake Gate, run the outlet-floor check against all 6 strict UTD24 outlets and rank: "Strongest fit: AMJ (no floor violations). Marginal at: SMJ (Dim 5 gap). Weak at: AMR (Dim 5 N/A, but Dim 2 below floor)." This becomes a partial input to `venue-fit` skill if the user wants outlet ranking.

### AMR special handling

AMR is the only UTD24 outlet that does not assess empirics. When the user's target is AMR:
- Replace Dim 5 sub-criteria with: (5.1) Logical consistency of the conceptual argument; (5.2) Boundary specification of proposed theory; (5.3) Generativity (does the theory generate testable propositions for future empirical work?); (5.4) Engagement with rival theoretical frameworks.
- Dim 4 ("Hypothesis Architecture") becomes "Proposition Architecture" — propositions instead of hypotheses, no HARKing check, no count constraint (AMR papers can have 5-10 propositions).
- Total still scored /100 but interpretation: AMR floor is **88+** for revise-and-resubmit prospects (AMR has the lowest acceptance rate in management, ~5-7%).

## Score interpretation (mirrors SKILL.md)

Apply **after** the outlet-conditioned floor check.

| Total | Reading (after outlet floor passes) |
|---|---|
| 90-100 | UTD24-ready at target outlet. Submit. Polishing only. |
| 80-89 | Send-out-for-review territory at target outlet. One focused revision needed before submission. |
| 65-79 | Major revision needed before UTD24 submission. Significant gaps in 1-2 dimensions. |
| 50-64 | Reposition to FT50-non-UTD24 outlet (JBR / LRP / APJM / MOR / SO) OR commit to 6-12 month rebuild. |
| <50 | Not UTD24 territory. Reframe project or accept different outlet tier. |

If total is in a "send out" band but an outlet floor is violated, the verdict downgrades one band (e.g., 85/100 with one outlet-floor violation → 75-84 band — "borderline, one focused revision before re-submission").
