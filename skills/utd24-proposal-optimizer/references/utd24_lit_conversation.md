# Dim 2 — Literature Conversation: parent-theory anchoring + contribution framing

This file deepens the four sub-criteria of Dim 2 in `utd24_rubric.md`. The companion reference is `utd24_strategy_innovation_entrepreneurship_lineages.md`, which lists the actual lineages.

---

## Why Dim 2 is the #1 UTD24 desk-reject driver

UTD24 reviewers and AEs are themselves embedded in specific conversations. They can tell within 60 seconds:

- Whether the paper has named a parent theory
- Whether the parent theory matches the argument being made
- Whether the contribution is a theoretical move (extension / integration / reconciliation / boundary / mechanism / new-theory) or a gap-fill claim
- Whether the recent anchor papers are central or peripheral

A paper that fails any of these reads as "this author hasn't engaged the literature" — which is the most common single reason for desk-reject at SMJ / AMJ / ASQ.

## Anchoring tests (Dim 2.1 + 2.2)

### Test A — Can you name the lineage in 3 words?

If the user cannot finish the sentence "this paper extends ___ ___ ___ theory" with a *specific* named lineage (not "the strategy literature"), Dim 2.1 ≤ 1.

Acceptable answers: "Adner-Kapoor ecosystem theory", "attention-based view", "behavioral theory of the firm", "Helfat-Peteraf dynamic managerial capabilities", "Csaszar-Gavetti cognitive strategy", etc.

Unacceptable: "strategic management theory", "innovation literature", "the resource-based perspective" (too broad without a specific variant).

### Test B — Are anchor papers central to the lineage?

For each anchor paper, ask:
- Does this paper *define* the lineage or substantially advance it?
- Is it cited by current UTD24 papers in the lineage?
- Was it published in a UTD24 outlet (or AMR / Strategy Science)?

Two anchor papers per lineage minimum. Three preferred. Mix of *foundational* (10+ years) and *recent* (last 5 years) is the UTD24 norm.

### Test C — Does the argument match the lineage?

Common mismatch patterns:
- Paper claims "RBV" but describes behavioral search → should be Carnegie / BTF
- Paper claims "dynamic capabilities" but describes governance choice → should be TCE
- Paper claims "ecosystems" but the focal firm is making purely internal decisions → should be RBV or strategic decision-making
- Paper claims "upper echelons" but the mechanism is structural / institutional, not cognitive → should be institutional theory or governance

When this mismatch is detected, recommend repositioning, not editing-around. Dim 2 score caps at 8/20 until repositioned.

## Contribution framing (Dim 2.3)

UTD24 papers claim contributions via *theoretical movement types*, not "filling a gap":

| Movement | What it is | Example claim phrase |
|---|---|---|
| EXTENSION | Adds a mediator / moderator / boundary that the parent theory had not specified | "We extend the attention-based view by specifying when attention focus is endogenous to..." |
| INTEGRATION | Joins two previously separate conversations with a defensible bridge | "We bridge the behavioral theory of the firm and dynamic capabilities by showing that..." |
| RECONCILIATION | Resolves a documented tension between published findings | "We reconcile the conflicting findings of X (2018) and Y (2021) by showing that..." |
| BOUNDARY | Specifies when the parent theory breaks down | "We identify a boundary condition for resource-based predictions of advantage when..." |
| MECHANISM | Specifies a previously unspecified causal pathway | "We open the black box of [theory's outcome] by identifying [mechanism]..." |
| NEW-THEORY | Builds new theory; rare; AMR-grade exposition required | "We propose a theory of..." |

**Phrases to flag and rewrite:**
- "We fill a gap in the literature" → no movement specified
- "No prior study has examined" → claim of primacy, requires evidence
- "We contribute to the literature on X" → which conversation, which move?
- "This paper advances our understanding of" → meaningless without naming the move

**Rewrite template:**
- BEFORE: "We contribute to the strategy literature by examining the role of CEO AI literacy in firm performance."
- AFTER: "We extend the Hambrick-Mason upper-echelons framework by identifying a previously unspecified moderator — CEO AI literacy — and specifying the mechanism (cognitive complementarity vs substitution between human and algorithmic judgment) through which it shapes the demographic-diversity → strategic-novelty relationship."

## Conversation visibility (Dim 2.4)

UTD24 papers don't just cite — they *converse*. Reader should be able to picture the conversation.

### Test D — Can you name 2-3 specific recent papers this paper is *arguing with*?

"Arguing with" can mean:
- Supporting one paper's finding while extending its scope
- Challenging one paper's finding with a counter-mechanism
- Reconciling two papers' conflicting findings
- Pointing out a boundary the cited paper did not consider

If the answer is "the paper cites 20 recent papers but distinguishes none", Dim 2.4 ≤ 1.

### Conversation visibility patterns

Pattern V1 — explicit interlocutor signaling:
> "Building on X (2022), who finds [result], but in contrast to Y (2023), who argues [result], we examine [our question]..."

Pattern V2 — interlocutor as foil:
> "Recent work by Z (2024) argues [position]. We agree that [partial], but show that [departure]..."

Pattern V3 — interlocutor as motivation:
> "The puzzle posed by W (2021) — why [phenomenon] — remains unresolved. We address it by..."

## REVIEW mode R1 attack patterns

R1 will attack on:

- **Parent theory unnamed**: "the authors do not clearly identify which theoretical conversation this paper joins; the introduction lists multiple literatures but commits to none"
- **Lineage mismatch**: "the authors frame this as a [claimed lineage] paper, but the argument and dependent variable belong to [actual lineage]; repositioning is required"
- **Gap-fill framing**: "the contribution is currently framed as 'filling a gap'; the authors must specify whether they extend, integrate, reconcile, bound, or mechanism-specify the parent theory"
- **Stale anchors**: "the only anchor papers are from 2005-2012; the field has moved substantially since [recent paper]; engagement with recent literature is required"
- **Generic citation lists**: "the introduction cites 15 papers as 'the literature on X' but engages none of them substantively"

---

## What scores ≥18/20 on Dim 2

The paper:
- Names a single specific parent theory in the lineage glossary
- Cites 2-3 anchor papers, at least one from last 5 years in a UTD24 outlet
- Frames contribution as a specific theoretical movement type with a verb (extends / integrates / reconciles / bounds / mechanism-specifies)
- Engages 2-3 specific recent papers as named interlocutors
- The contribution claim is one that another senior researcher in the lineage would recognize and could agree or disagree with

What scores 0-8/20: any of "the strategy literature", "fill a gap", no named anchor, anchor papers all 15+ years old.
